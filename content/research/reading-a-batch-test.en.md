Every batch we ship has a report from an independent laboratory. Here's what the four main lines mean.

## Microbial limits (ISO 17516)

Confirms the product is within the accepted limits for bacteria, yeast and mould at the time of release. "Pass" means all counts were within specification.

## Preservative efficacy (ISO 11930)

The "challenge test". The product is deliberately inoculated with microorganisms and monitored over 28 days to show the preservative system works through normal use.

## pH

Measured at release. We publish the range on each product page; a result inside that range is a pass.

## Stability (accelerated)

The product is stored at 40 °C and 75% relative humidity for three months to simulate a longer shelf life. Colour, odour, viscosity and separation are checked against a retained reference sample.
