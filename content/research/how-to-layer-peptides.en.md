Peptides are forgiving. They sit happily next to most actives, which makes them easy to add to a routine you already like.

## A simple order

1. Cleanse.
2. Water-based serums, thinnest first (Argireline, then Matrixyl).
3. Eye cream.
4. Moisturiser.
5. SPF in the morning.

## AM / PM split

- **Morning:** peptide serum, moisturiser, SPF. Vitamin C can go before the peptide serum; let it absorb for a minute.
- **Evening:** retinoid first on dry skin, wait a few minutes, then peptide serum or cream. Many people prefer a copper peptide cream on retinoid nights because of its comfortable texture.

## The one to separate

Copper peptides and strong direct acids (glycolic, high-strength ascorbic acid) in the same step can reduce the stability of the copper complex. Use them at different times of day rather than layering them directly.
